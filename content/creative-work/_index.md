      - type: "Stage play"
        status: "Writer"
        title: "*Fletcher Presiding*"
        description: |-
          A comedy of manners and errors about bad decision-making: the Wellington Harbour Board during the 1913 waterfront strike.

          It is written as a choose-your-own-adventure. The board is deadlocked and the audience makes three decisions about how it plays out. Those decisions lead to one of three endings: the actual outcome, or one of two alternative histories, both of which have been speculated about in research.

          It was first written as a musical and entered in the 2017 Wellington Fringe Festival, then withdrawn before the season. The script stands and can be staged as a straight play.
